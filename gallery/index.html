Case Studies

Real Events We've Delivered

Eight branding projects for named clients, each mapped from objective to outcome — see more awards-specific detail on Award Ceremonies & Branding.

APM UK — 25th Anniversary

APM UK — 25th Anniversary Celebration

Objective
Mark APM UK's 25th anniversary with a celebration that felt genuinely milestone, not just another corporate dinner.
Activation
End-to-end venue branding tying the anniversary identity together, from arrival through to the photo moment.
Delivery
Branded entrance pillars and a purple-and-white balloon arch, installed and staged for the evening.
Outcomes
A cohesive anniversary atmosphere with branding carried through every guest touchpoint.
Vodafone Business — VIP Lounge

Vodafone Business — VIP Lounge Experience

Objective
Give VIP guests and sponsors a dedicated space that felt distinct from the main event floor.
Activation
A fully branded VIP lounge with laser-cut panel detailing and a matching illuminated centrepiece.
Delivery
Custom-built lounge branding and an illuminated table centrepiece, installed for the evening's VIP guests.
Outcomes
A premium environment that gave VIP guests and sponsors a space worth spending time in.
LFC Foundation — Charity Gala

LFC Foundation — Charity Gala Centrepiece

Objective
Give a charity gala dinner a visual centrepiece that reflected the Foundation's identity.
Activation
An illuminated centrepiece topped with a Liverpool FC crested football, designed as the evening's focal point.
Delivery
Custom-built illuminated football centrepieces installed across the dinner tables.
Outcomes
A polished, on-brand dinner setting that reinforced the Foundation's identity throughout the evening.
Clatterbridge Cancer Charity

Clatterbridge Cancer Charity — Fundraising Dinner

Objective
Support a cancer charity fundraising dinner with branding that kept the cause visible without overwhelming the evening.
Activation
An illuminated heart-logo centrepiece carrying the charity's branding to every table.
Delivery
Custom-built illuminated centrepieces installed for the fundraising dinner.
Outcomes
A warm, on-brand dinner setting that kept the charity's identity present throughout the evening.
Irish Post Awards — Red Carpet

Irish Post Awards — Red Carpet Entrance

Objective
Give finalists and guests a proper red-carpet arrival moment worth photographing.
Activation
A branded step-and-repeat wall paired with illuminated entrance pillars along the arrival route.
Delivery
A branded press wall and matching entrance pillars, installed for arrivals and media photography.
Outcomes
A red-carpet arrival that generated press and guest photography from the moment doors opened.
JD Foundation

JD Foundation — Charity Awards Evening

Objective
Bring the JD Foundation's "changing lives" message to life physically at their awards evening.
Activation
A themed step-and-repeat wall carrying the Foundation's branding into every photo taken on the night.
Delivery
A custom-built, jungle-themed branded wall installed as the evening's photo backdrop.
Outcomes
A backdrop that put the Foundation's mission in front of every guest, and every photo shared afterwards.
Investment Week Awards

Investment Week — Fund Manager Of The Year Awards

Objective
Give a City awards ceremony a stage presence that matched the seriousness of the occasion.
Activation
A branded stage backdrop built around the Fund Manager of the Year identity.
Delivery
A custom stage backdrop, installed and staged for the ceremony.
Outcomes
A stage that carried sponsor and event branding through every announcement and photo of the night.
IRIS Customer Awards

IRIS Customer Awards 2020 — Table Centrepiece

Objective
Give every table a branded, on-theme centrepiece for the ceremony.
Activation
An illuminated light cube, branded to the event and numbered for table seating.
Delivery
A light cube centrepiece produced and placed at every table for the evening.
Outcomes
Consistent event branding on every table throughout the ceremony.
Staff Excellence Awards

Staff Excellence Awards — Clatterbridge NHS

Objective
Recognise NHS staff with an awards evening that felt as significant as the people it celebrated.
Activation
A branded illuminated backdrop welcoming staff into a properly staged awards evening.
Delivery
An illuminated branded backdrop, installed for the ceremony red carpet.
Outcomes
A memorable arrival moment for NHS staff attending their own recognition evening.
