How It Works

A Photo Moment Built To Be Shared

From AI-generated portraits to branded photo and video booths, every format is designed around one moment: a visitor stops, engages, and leaves with something worth sharing. Branding, prompts and outputs are all tailored to your campaign.

  • AI-generated portraits, filters and branded overlays
  • Instant digital delivery via QR code, email or social share

  • Fully brandable for sponsors and seasonal campaigns
