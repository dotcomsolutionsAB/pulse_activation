How It Works

One Format, Reinvented Every Season

Seasonal Experiences take our Quest, Lockbox and digital game mechanics and rebuild them around the calendar — Easter Hunts in spring, Summer trails through the holidays, Halloween Experiences in autumn, Christmas Trails in winter. Same proven formats, a new story each time.

  • Easter, Summer, Halloween and Christmas formats
  • Reuses proven Quest, Lockbox and digital game mechanics
  • Fully rebranded artwork, prizes and messaging each season
  • Scaled to single-zone or venue-wide activations

Seasonal Formats

Built Around Your Calendar

From a single seasonal weekend to a multi-week campaign — each season reuses our proven Quest, Lockbox and Digital Games mechanics, rebranded around what's coming up next.

Easter

Easter

Trail-based egg hunts with brandable clues, checkpoints and prizes — built to extend the Easter half-term visit.

Example: Egg Quest — a QR-code Easter egg hunt across a shopping centre, with checkpoints hidden in every unit.
Explore Quest
Summer

Summer

Long dwell-time trails and outdoor games built for peak footfall through the summer holidays.

Example: A summer holiday trail with digital games at every checkpoint, keeping families on-site for longer.

Halloween

Halloween

Themed trail and puzzle formats built for spooky-season footfall, from single-zone scares to venue-wide trails.

Example: A Halloween Lockbox trail — solve spooky clues to unlock the final prize box.

Christmas

Festive story trails designed to extend visits through the busiest trading season of the year.
