How It Works

One Format, Reinvented Every Season

Seasonal Experiences take our Quest, Lockbox and digital game mechanics and rebuild them around the calendar — Easter Hunts in spring, Christmas Trails in winter, Halloween Experiences in autumn. Same proven formats, a new story each time.

  • Easter Hunts, Christmas Trails and Halloween Experiences
  • Reuses proven Quest, Lockbox and digital game mechanics
  • Fully rebranded artwork, prizes and messaging each season
  • Scaled to single-zone or venue-wide activations
