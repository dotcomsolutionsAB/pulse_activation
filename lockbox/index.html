Turn Engagement Into Reward.

Create anticipation. Capture attention. Reward participation. Lockbox is an interactive prize activation designed to turn audience participation into an exciting branded moment — combining a simple game mechanic with the excitement of an instant reward.

How It Works

How Lockbox Works

Participants are invited to enter for the opportunity to unlock a prize. The experience can begin through a fully branded digital entry page, accessed through your website, social media, email campaign or QR codes positioned throughout a venue or event.

From registration through to the prize reveal, the journey can be customised around your brand, campaign and audience.

Watch A Lockbox Activation
The Journey

Five Steps From Entry To Reward

01

Attract

Use digital campaigns, QR codes, screens, signage or event staff to invite people to participate.

02

Engage

Participants access a branded experience and enter their details or complete the required campaign action.

03

Unlock

Selected participants receive the opportunity to unlock the Lockbox and discover whether they've won.

04

Reward

Prizes can range from branded merchandise and vouchers to VIP experiences, tickets, products or sponsor rewards.

05

Measure

Where required, the activation can support data capture and campaign reporting, giving brands greater insight into participation and engagement.

Digital Or Physical

Digital Or Physical — You Choose

Lockbox can work as a digital experience or become a physical centrepiece within a live activation. For physical environments, the experience can be incorporated into bespoke branded structures such as lockers, cabinets, display units or themed installations — creating a highly visible focal point that attracts attention and builds anticipation around the prize reveal.

Why Lockbox

More Than A Prize Mechanic

Lockbox can help campaigns:

  • Increase footfall and participation
  • Capture opted-in customer or fan data
  • Drive visitors to a website or landing page
  • Support product launches and promotions
  • Create memorable prize moments
  • Increase sponsor visibility
  • Encourage social sharing
  • Generate measurable audience engagement
Branding

Built Around Your Brand

Every Lockbox activation can be tailored to the campaign. Branding can extend across the digital entry journey, physical installation, screens, signage, prizes and supporting promotional materials — creating one consistent experience from first interaction to final reward.

Sponsors

Sponsor Opportunities

Lockbox provides a strong platform for sponsor integration. Sponsors can be incorporated into the entry journey, physical installation, prizes, digital screens and winner moments — giving partners a more engaging presence than traditional logo placement alone.
