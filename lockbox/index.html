How It Works

Escape-Room Energy, Built Into Your Venue

Teams work through a sequence of physical and digital clues to crack codes, unlock boxes and beat the clock. It's competitive, social and endlessly shareable — and every puzzle is designed around your brand, venue and available time slot.

  • Physical lockboxes, digital clue apps, or a hybrid of both
  • Difficulty and duration tuned to your audience
  • Leaderboards and team competition built in
  • Fully brandable puzzles, props and rewards
