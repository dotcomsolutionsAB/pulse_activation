Case Studies

A Closer Look At Our Work

Nine activations, mapped from brief to outcome — across retail, sport, hospitality, waterfront, awards and culture.

Shopping Centre Trail

Shopping Centre Trail

Challenge
Drive footfall and dwell time across the centre during a quieter trading period.
Idea
A story-driven Quest trail branded to the centre, designed to route visitors past every unit.
Delivery
Branded trail signage, a mobile-led Quest experience and on-site staff support across a multi-week campaign.
Results
Longer average visits, and a trail visitors wanted to finish and share.
Media
Full activation video, on-site photography and social content captured throughout the campaign.
Matchday Fan Zone

Matchday Fan Zone

Challenge
Give matchday fans something to do beyond the concourse queues.
Idea
A branded fan zone activation combining interactive games with sponsor integration.
Delivery
A concourse-based fan zone, built and staffed for every home matchday across the season.
Results
A more engaged crowd, and genuine sponsor visibility inside the experience.
Media
Matchday highlight reel and fan-generated content from the activation.
Waterfront Guest Trail

Waterfront Guest Trail

Challenge
Turn passing waterfront visitors into active participants, not just passers-by.
Idea
An interactive guest trail woven into the marina's existing walkways and berths.
Delivery
A self-guided Quest experience with waterfront-branded touchpoints, launched across peak season.
Results
Visitors explored more of the waterfront, and left with an experience worth sharing.
Media
Guest experience video and photography from across the season.
Award Evening Branding

Award Evening Branding

Challenge

A cohesive, premium evening with sponsor branding present at every guest touchpoint.
Media
Stage reveal and evening atmosphere video, plus photography from the night.
