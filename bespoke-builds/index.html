How It Works

Custom, From The Ground Up

When a brief doesn't fit neatly into Quest, Lockbox, AI & Photo or any of our other formats, we design a bespoke build from first principles — combining mechanics, technology and production to match exactly what your venue and audience need.

  • Concept design built entirely around your brief
  • Combines mechanics from across our existing formats, or introduces new ones
  • Full production, branding and technical build
  • Scaled to any venue size or audience
