Before You Get In Touch

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I hear back after I get in touch?+
We normally respond within one working day. If it's urgent, calling 07352 403080 directly is the fastest route.
Can I speak to someone before sending a full brief?+
Yes — you can book a 20-minute discovery call using the link on this page, or send whatever detail you have through the form below and we'll come back with next steps and questions.
Do you publish set prices or package rates?+
No. Every activation depends on venue, scale, timings and sponsor or partner involvement, so we scope and quote each project individually once we understand what you need — nothing on this site is a published rate.
Which industries and venues do you work with?+
Shopping centres, sports & stadiums, hotels & resorts, marinas & waterfronts, visitor attractions, museums, agencies & brands, and award ceremony & sponsor branding — see the Industries menu above for the full list of sectors we design for.
What if I need something outside your standard experience formats?+
That's what our Bespoke Builds service is for — fully custom activations designed from scratch around your brand, venue and campaign rather than one of our standard formats.
