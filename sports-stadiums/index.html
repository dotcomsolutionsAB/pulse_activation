Examples In Practice

Sponsor Integration & Fan Engagement, Combined

Concrete examples of how sponsor visibility and fan engagement work inside the same mechanic — not bolted on separately.

Sponsor-Branded Fan Hunt

Example: A pre-match Fan Hunt branded entirely around a headline sponsor, with checkpoints hidden across the concourse and a sponsor-funded prize at the final stop.

AI Fan Booth

Example: Supporters generate a sponsor-branded AI portrait pitch-side, shared instantly to their phone and social channels — fan-made content that carries the sponsor's branding every time it's shared.

Half-Time Sponsor Challenge

Example: A big-screen reaction game sponsored by a headline partner, played live by fans pulled from the crowd during the half-time interval.
