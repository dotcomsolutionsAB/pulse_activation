What Activations Do For Your Venue

A waterfront trail gives berth-holders and day visitors a reason to explore beyond the pontoons — past the retail, dining and events they might otherwise walk straight by. Each checkpoint captures visitor data and preferences, which feed the offers and event invitations that bring that same visitor back throughout the season.

Examples In Practice

Built For Every Kind Of Waterfront

The same mechanics, adapted to the destination — from a marina village to a berth-holder community.

Coastal Marina Village

Example: A family discovery trail routing visitors past retail units and waterside dining, with a stamp card redeemable at a berth-side café — driving weekend footfall beyond the marina entrance.

Riverside Waterfront Development

Example: A seasonal quest tied to the waterfront's events calendar, capturing visitor data used to re-target attendees ahead of the next event.

Berth-Holder Community

Example: A loyalty-linked activation rewarding returning berth-holders with early access to waterfront events and offers, strengthening retention across the sailing season.
