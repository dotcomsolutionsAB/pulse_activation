How It Works

Interactive, Shareable, Built To Perform

From giant motion-sensor screens to head-to-head touchscreen challenges, our digital games are built to stop passers-by and turn them into players — capturing attention, data and social content in the process.

  • Motion-sensor, touchscreen and app-based formats
  • Real-time leaderboards to drive repeat play
  • Built-in data capture and social sharing prompts
  • Fully brandable interfaces, rewards and prize logic
