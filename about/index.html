About Pulse Activations

Every Activation Is Built To Perform.

Who we are, how we work, and the partners who help us deliver — from first concept through to measurable results.

Founder Photo
Who's Behind Pulse

Oliver Couldridge, Founder

Oliver founded Pulse Activations to bring strategic thinking and hands-on delivery to experiential activations — treating every brief as a business problem to solve, not just an event to run. That means starting with your audience and goals, designing a concept that's genuinely customised to your venue and sponsors, and staying involved through delivery and reporting so results are measured, not assumed.

  • Direct point of contact from first call to final report
  • A specialist delivery network, brought in to match your brief
  • Every activation reported against the outcomes that mattered

Partners

Delivered With Specialist Production, Software & Fulfilment Partners

Pulse Activations designs and manages every brief end to end, but we don't try to be everything ourselves. Physical production (builds, branding and staging), the software behind digital and data-capture experiences, and on-the-ground fulfilment are each supported by specialist delivery partners where appropriate — brought in to match the scale and complexity of your brief, and always managed under Pulse Activations from first concept through to on-site delivery and reporting.
